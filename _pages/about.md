I am a medical student at Emory University School of Medicine with interests in cutaneous oncology, health equity, and elucidating the pathogenesis of rare and understudied diseases.

Before medical school, I worked as a research fellow in the Dermatology Branch of the National Institutes of Health, where I studied Merkel cell carcinoma, a rare and particularily aggressive skin cancer. Prior to that, I did basic lung cancer research at McGill and worked on projects involving CRISPR-based therapeutics and computational genomics at Harvard University.
